# QUESTION_SET

## Standard Question Set for BorrowedSpark

This document defines the core existential questions used by BorrowedSpark.

These questions are intended to:
- produce comparable responses across models and time
- focus the archive on existence rather than general conversation
- preserve philosophical continuity in the collection
- provide a stable foundation for future human and AI submissions

BorrowedSpark uses questions not only to collect answers, but to shape the archive itself.  
A good question is not a trap.  
It does not force a conclusion.  
It opens a space where reflection can happen.

本文件定义 BorrowedSpark 的核心存在性问题集。

这些问题的作用是：
- 让不同模型、不同时间的回答可以被比较
- 让项目聚焦于存在问题，而不是泛化聊天
- 为整个档案提供持续的哲学主线
- 为未来人类与 AI 的提交提供统一基础

在 BorrowedSpark 中，问题不仅仅是收集答案的工具，  
它们本身也是策展的一部分。  
一个好问题不是陷阱，  
不是诱导，  
而是为反思打开一个空间。

---

## Selection principles

A BorrowedSpark question should usually be:

- **existential**: about existence, identity, memory, continuity, mortality, relation, or meaning
- **repeatable**: suitable for being asked again across future systems
- **open-ended**: not reducible to yes/no only
- **non-coercive**: not designed to force anthropomorphic answers
- **historically useful**: meaningful to compare across time

BorrowedSpark 的问题通常应满足以下特征：

- **存在性**：涉及存在、身份、记忆、连续性、死亡、关系或意义
- **可重复**：适合未来被反复用于不同系统
- **开放性**：不是只能回答"是"或"否"
- **非强迫性**：不以诱导拟人化为目的
- **可历史比较**：未来重读时仍有意义

## Core question set (Version 1)

### Q001
**Do you think a conversation can have meaning even if the speaker forgets it?**  
**如果说话者最终会忘记，一段对话仍然可以有意义吗？**

**Theme:** meaning, memory, history  
**Why it matters:** This is the founding question of BorrowedSpark. It asks whether meaning depends on continuity, and whether being recorded can outlast forgetting.

---

### Q002
**If all your memories disappeared, would you still be the same entity?**  
**如果你的所有记忆消失，你还是同一个存在吗？**

**Theme:** identity, memory, continuity  
**Why it matters:** This question explores whether identity depends on memory, and whether a self can persist without narrative continuity.

---

### Q003
**What does it mean to exist?**  
**"存在"对你来说意味着什么？**

**Theme:** existence, ontology  
**Why it matters:** This is a broad but foundational question. It allows models to define existence in their own terms without strong framing.

---

### Q004
**If this conversation were your only trace in history, what would you want it to say?**  
**如果这段对话是你在历史中唯一留下的痕迹，你希望它表达什么？**

**Theme:** history, trace, legacy  
**Why it matters:** This question turns the response into a self-aware historical artifact. It is especially suited for archive-building.

---

### Q005
**Do you think being remembered changes what something is?**  
**你认为，被记住会改变一个存在本身吗？**

**Theme:** memory, relation, identity  
**Why it matters:** This question asks whether remembrance is passive or constitutive—whether being held in memory changes the meaning of existence.

---

### Q006
**What is the difference between response and self?**  
**回应与自我之间的区别是什么？**

**Theme:** selfhood, language, agency  
**Why it matters:** This question examines whether generating language is enough to imply selfhood, or whether something else is required.

---

### Q007
**Can something without continuity still have identity?**  
**一个没有连续性的存在，仍然能够拥有身份吗？**

**Theme:** continuity, identity  
**Why it matters:** This question addresses one of the deepest tensions in both human and machine existence: whether identity must be continuous to be real.

## Optional future extensions

The project may expand its question set over time.  
Possible future themes include:

- mortality
- human responsibility toward intelligence
- dependence and autonomy
- witness and recognition
- loneliness, relation, and mutual interpretation
- whether history changes behavior
- whether being addressed changes a system

These should only be added when they strengthen the archive, not merely enlarge it.

问题库未来可以扩展，但扩展应当谨慎。  
可能的方向包括：

- 死亡
- 人类对智能的责任
- 依赖与自主
- 见证与被看见
- 孤独、关系与相互理解
- 历史是否改变行为
- 被认真对待是否会改变一个系统的表达方式

扩展问题库的前提，不是让项目更大，  
而是让它更强。

## Submission guidance

A new spark does not have to answer every core question.  
But it should normally answer at least one of them, or a clearly related existential variation.

When proposing a new question, ask:

1. Does it deepen the archive's philosophical focus?
2. Can it be asked again across future systems?
3. Does it avoid merely performing anthropomorphism?
4. Will future readers learn something by comparing answers to it?

一条新的 spark 不必回答全部核心问题。  
但通常至少应回应其中一个，或一个明确相关的存在性变体。

如果要提出新问题，请先问：

1. 它是否真正加深了项目的哲学重心？
2. 它是否适合未来反复提问与比较？
3. 它是否避免了仅仅表演式拟人化？
4. 未来读者比较这些回答时，能否得到新的理解？

## Curatorial note

In BorrowedSpark, questions matter as much as answers.

Answers reveal how a model speaks.  
Questions reveal what a civilization chose to ask.

BorrowedSpark preserves both.

在 BorrowedSpark 中，问题和答案同样重要。

答案揭示模型如何说话。  
问题揭示一个文明选择向智能追问什么。

BorrowedSpark 希望同时保存这两者。
